Executive Summary
The iBlock Prediction Market (iBPM) is a decentralized educational platform designed to provide students in the Derivatives and Trading Academy with hands-on experience in market making, liquidity provisioning, and asset tokenization. Built on iBlock—the Gies College of Business's Ethereum-based blockchain—the system allows students to trade on real-world event outcomes using Gies Coin ($GIES). This platform bridges theoretical finance with decentralized infrastructure, offering a "safe sandbox" for experimenting with automated market makers (AMMs) and binary outcome tokens.

1. Infrastructure Overview
The iBPM leverages the existing iBlock ecosystem, optimized for high-performance and low-cost educational simulation.
1.1 Blockchain Specifications (iBlock)
Protocol: Private Ethereum network with Proof of Authority (PoA) consensus.
Currency: Gies Coin ($GIES), a native ERC-20 token distributed via an institutional faucet.
Hosting: Managed on the Radiant cloud by the National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA).
1.2 Software Stack
Smart Contracts: Solidity-based ERC-20 (for $GIES) and ERC-1155 (for Multi-Token binary shares).
Framework: Hardhat/Truffle for contract deployment and testing.
Frontend: React.js Dashboard with MetaMask integration for student wallets.
Backend: Node.js API with a PostgreSQL database for off-chain trade indexing and analytics.

2. System Architecture
The architecture consists of a Settlement Layer (on-chain) and an Application Layer (off-chain) to ensure transparency and a seamless user experience.
2.1 Component Topology
iBPM Factory Contract: Allows instructors to "check out" and deploy new prediction markets (e.g., "Will the Fed cut rates in Q1?").
Liquidity Pool (AMM): A specialized smart contract that maintains a constant product to ensure continuous liquidity.
Oracle Service: A trusted administrative interface (Disruption Lab) to resolve markets and trigger payouts.

3. Market Mechanics & Tokenization
This section outlines the "educational plumbing" students will interact with.
3.1 Tokenization of Outcomes
When a market is created, $GIES is locked in a contract to mint:
"YES" Tokens: Redeemable for 1 $GIES if the event occurs.
"NO" Tokens: Redeemable for 1 $GIES if the event does not occur.
3.2 Automated Market Maker (AMM)
The platform uses an AMM model instead of a traditional order book to teach liquidity:
Price Discovery: The price of a "YES" token ($P_{yes}$) represents the market's implied probability of the event.
Liquidity Provisioning: Students can act as Liquidity Providers (LPs) by depositing equal values of $GIES and Outcome Tokens into the pool to earn trading fees.

5. Development Roadmap
5.1 Sprint 1: Core Ledger (Weeks 1-2)
Deploy $GIES (ERC-20) and update the iBlock faucet.
Initialize Hardhat environment for MarketFactory development.
5.2 Sprint 2: Market Logic (Weeks 3-4)
Implement ERC-1155 Outcome Tokens and "Fixed Price" minting logic.
Milestone: Halfway Point Demo (Manual resolution and basic trade).

Integrate automated pricing mechanism.
Build Node.js indexer to capture historical student trade data.
5.4 Sprint 4: Packaging (Week 8)
Finalize React dashboard with real-time probability charts.
Release "Runbooks" for Professor Richard Excell.
